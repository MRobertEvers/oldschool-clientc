Nothing here has been watched. Every number below is a narrator's claim to be checked against the cache, Blert or a frame count before it is encoded. Disagreements are in the last section.

## Where two guides disagree

- Javelin special: Lilratbags CbCA86C_P2E [02:09] "every four attacks" against King Condor 1pAStJfQzvc [09:22] and Sun fish M7sIf6mx4vw [12:52] "every five attacks". Two of three say five; none is a measurement. The cache and Blert settle it.
- Reinforcement time: 40 s (Sun fish, Pen Sir, Reynold, MarlGames, Proudest Dad) against 45 s (Lilratbags CbCA86C_P2E [03:14] and [06:33]). Five sources to one; Lilratbags may count from the moment the arena opens instead of from the wave start (his own [04:53] says to move five ticks, 3 seconds after clicking continue).
- Equipment grab click window: four ticks (Pen Sir [39:12], Sun fish [59:44]) against five ticks (MarlGames RKz7Vs3lElU [09:40], "you have five ticks to click on it", in the context of a sound plugin).
- Sol Heredit parry prayer window: tickniques xn7j4qQsNk0 "second tick of each three tick attack cycle"; Pen Sir QvqrKrLVF1Q [39:47] "turn on protect from melee three ticks after he starts charging". These read as the same fact in different frames of reference (his animation start versus his hit); a frame count decides.
