## Verdict on the rig pass's candidate rows (what the recorder confirms)

Method: an `NPC_ATTACK` row of (npc id, attack) exists only when the client set that animation id on that npc, so a count above 0 in the "seen" column is an OBSERVATION that the npc plays the animation. It identifies the animation as that npc's attack marker; it does not give the hit tick, damage or style beyond the plugin's name.

* **Attested by recordings (animation played on that npc id, in at least 9 of 18 runs):** jaguar 10847 (`attack1`, tier `name`), serpent shaman 10859 (`attack1`, `name`), minotaur 10843 (`attack1`, `rig+name`; also played by npc 12813), Fremennik archer 10850 / seer 10853 / berserker 10856 (`attack`, `name`), javelin colossus 10892 (`attack1`) and 10893 (`attack2`), shockwave colossus 10903 (`attack1`), Sol Heredit 10883 (`attack2`), 10884 (`attack3`), 10885 (`attack4`), 10887 (`attack6`), manticore 10869 (`attack1`). Every `role` in the rig tsv for these ids already says attack; the recorder removes the "candidate" doubt for the (npc id, animation id) pairing only.
* **Seen rarely:** Sol 10884 (grapple) 11 events in 9 runs and 10887 (triple attack) 10 events in 9 runs; Fremennik berserker 10856 34 events in 12 runs (the berserker is the first to die).
* **Not confirmed by this source:** Sol 10882 (`..._01_melee_attack`) and 10886 (`..._tripleattack`, 12 ticks) are on the rig, never in the plugin table; the recorder says nothing about whether Sol plays them. Manticore 10868 (`..._triple_charge`) is a read window, never an event. No recorded Sol animation outside the four in the table.
* **Spotanims and graphics (cache names agree with the plugin's use; no recorded event proves the id):** 2681 / 2683 / 2685 are `vfx_manticore_01_projectile_magic|ranged|melee_01`, matching the plugin's mage / range / melee; 2689-2691 `..._crystal_charge_01_beam_01-03` (laser SCAN) and 2693-2695 `..._crystal_attack_01_beam_01-03` (laser SHOT) match the plugin's phase names (the rig tsv's npc is `colosseum_beam_crystal` 12824 = the laser prism); 2669-2671 are `spotanim_colossi_finalboss_01-03_melee` (the plugin's Sol dust), 2698 `vfx_colosseum_sunfire_lightning_01_beam_01` (the plugin's Sol pool), 2687 `vfx_colosseum_totem_projectile_01` (healing totem heal). In the sample the dust (306 events), lasers (141) and pools (99) fired, so ids 2669-2671, 2689-2695 and 2698 are SEEN as graphics objects; 2687 never produced a `COLOSSEUM_TOTEM_HEAL` (0 events though 10 totems spawned): do not cite it as confirmed.
