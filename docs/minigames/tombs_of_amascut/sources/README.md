## Video guides (machine transcripts)

Downloaded 2026-10-02 with `yt-dlp --skip-download --write-auto-subs --sub-langs "en-orig,en" --sub-format vtt --write-info-json` (4 s apart; YouTube answered HTTP 429 on the secondary `en` track for 9 ids, the `en-orig` track and the info json were still obtained, the json by a second metadata-only call) and converted by `tools/raid_gate/vtt_to_md.py --raid "Tombs of Amascut"` to `transcripts/yt_<id>.md`. Auto-captions mangle numbers and names ("zabak", "kafri", "akka", "baba"); cross-check before use.

**Overlay column.** *Not watched*: titles, descriptions, chapters and captions only. `stated` means the title, description or captions name a tick counter, attack timer, metronome or tick overlay; blank means nothing in the text says so and the spec worker must open the video (`tools/raid_gate/frame_count.py`) to find out. A named overlay is a lead, not a measurement. Of 40 videos, **only one states one in its text**: Tellacon's Wardens guide (captions: "attack timer"). Videos known from the text to use the Radius Markers plugin (SoundsRS, Akkha 3:49) are not tick overlays.
**Tick-talk timestamps**: caption paragraphs where the speaker says an N-tick figure (mm:ss). **Attack/mechanic-name mentions**: caption lines (at least 30 s apart, first 16) that name the room's attacks or mechanics, as anchors for where a room's attacks are on screen; keyword lists are per room, so expect noise. Chapters are the uploader's own. fps is the source stream's.
