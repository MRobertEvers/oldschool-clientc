(4) Where our content stops a raider (a door that will not open by click, a chest that sells nothing or the wrong thing, a room that does not hand over to the next, a HUD or music row that differs from its raid-wide spec row), settle it from the row's source line and fix the content; shared scripts (tob.rs2, tob_raid.rs2, tob_spectate.rs2, tob_rewards.rs2 and the lobby scripts) belong to the seam whose half of the raid the code serves: lobby, party, door, the passages and chests up to Nylocas's exit are the first seam's; from Sotetseg's entrance to the vault, rewards and the exit the second's. (7) Each seam ends with: its scratch relay half under build/seam_state/<pass>/ that runs green twice under two run names, a RECIPE section for DRIVER_NOTES.md (the kit slot by slot, what is bought at each chest, the per-room kill plan in ten lines each, the clicks for every door and passage), the list of raid-wide rows its half can measure with the read for each, and every content bug with file:line.
