## **STAT REQUIREMENTS**

(With the exception of specific account builds)

Keep in mind that these are absolute **MINIMUM**. You may find that people will not raid with you if you just have the bare minimum in stats and gear. That said, if you are only just learning we will try to cater to you during our workshops.

**75+ Melee stats **

**70+ Prayer**

** 75+ Mage **

**75+ Range

**

Ideally, if you plan to keep raiding consistently after you have been taught, you will want:

**80+ Attack**

**90+ Strength

80+ Defence

90+ Range

90+ Mage

77+ Prayer

**

While you will not need them when **learning** in teams, you should also aim to achieve:

**70+ Farming **

**78+ Herblore **

**60+ Construction**

Other useful stats for some rooms are:

**Thieving (Thieving room)

Woodcutting (Muttadile, Ice Demon)

Mining (Guardians)

Agility (Tightrope)**

Improving these stats will speed up the rooms listed next to them.

## **GEAR REQUIREMENTS**

### Minimum gear requirements

If you cannot manage to get the following items then expect to be refused when you ask to join a raid with a mentor (or others). If you cannot properly equip yourself for end-game content then it isn’t our responsibility to carry you through it.

 Barrows Gloves

 Salve (ei)

 God Cape (i)

 Fire Cape

 Dragon Defender

 Dragon Sword

 Trident of the Swamp

 Abyssal Tentacle

 Toxic Blowpipe

 Occult Necklace

 Berserker Ring (i)

 Amulet of Fury

 Bandos Godsword

You are **strongly advised** to obtain the following before starting to Raid as they are easy DPS upgrades.

 Ava’s Assembler

 Fighter Torso
