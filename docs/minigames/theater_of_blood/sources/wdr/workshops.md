## **OFFICIAL WORKSHOPS**

Workshops are a weekly community activity where multiple Mentors and Experienced raiders make themselves available to raid with and teach the newer raiders. We split all the learners up into teams with one mentor and a few experienced raiders, and the beginners will be taught how to raid. Anyone is welcome to these events, and updates are posted in #announcements regarding what you should do a few hours prior to these events.

- If you think you’re too terrible to be taught and think that the mentors will get angry at you then rest assured you are not the only one. Everyone is bad when they first start. Mentors are used to this and are there to help you learn. As long as you listen and follow instructions then you don’t need to worry about anything else.

- You do not need to know a world or cc as these will be decided once you split up into teams. There are announcements close to when the workshops start with extra information and instructions.

- Specific workshop channels will be opened up once the announcements are made a few hours prior to the starting time. Any other information you need will be covered in the announcements.

Workshops begin at 6pm Saturday UTC.

A countdown to the next workshop and timezone conversions can be found on the side of the page.
