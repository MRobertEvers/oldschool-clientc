Source: RSProx index 4562..4565, revision 242, world 386, recorded 2026-11-12/13: one full
playthrough by a male character. Every line below is the server's own page text (`<br>`
joined back into one line, the speaker from the chathead). `[n]` is the tick in the session.

Compared against `Transcript:Monkey Madness I` revision 15382623 (2026-10-26).

The whole page with every change applied is `monkey_madness_i_wiki_new.wikitext` beside this
file (93,484 characters, 18 hunks). It has been entered into the wiki's source editor, with
an edit summary, by the signed-in account, and **not saved**: the tab was left open on the
edit form. The comparison was loose (text containment after stripping markup), so "not on
the page" was only acted on where the page holds a near-identical line or an existing
section that the recorded line belongs in.

## Seen in the recording and left alone

- Zooknock's reply to each hand-in was "Nicely done." four times and "Good work." once
  (4563 [668], [699], [708], [715], [5029]); the page has "Well done, human.". It may be a
  roll among several; not changed.
- The other atoll crates (bananas, scimitars, tinderboxes, thread, needles, chisels,
  hammers, gnome eyes) use the same single box; the page has no sections for them.
- Lines that belong on NPC transcript pages, which the page's own header points to, were
  not added here: Padulah, Dugopul, Hafuba, Lofu and Denadu, Daga and the other
  shopkeepers, the monkey minder's small talk, the monkey in the backpack, the Monkey's
  Aunt and Uncle as a monkey, the Elder Guard ("Move!" / "Grrr ... Get out of my way."),
  the jail guards ("Grr ... Get out of my way..."), the monkey archer, Oobapohk.
- Gendered lines the page already brackets ("[him/her/them]") match the recording's male
  form.
- The chapter cards and the "Meanwhile…" lines are an interface (`mm_message`), not
  message boxes; the page's `qact` rows already describe them as actions.
