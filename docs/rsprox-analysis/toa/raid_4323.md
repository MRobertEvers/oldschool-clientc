# Index 4323: a solo Expert raid at level 310 (rev 241, 2026-10-22)

The owner's own raid on world 471: every room, Apmeken → Ba-Ba → Scabaras → Kephri → Het →
Akkha → Crondis → Zebak → the Wardens. The index files it as "Sorceress's Garden", which is
the world's activity, not the raid. It is the first normal-world rev-241 raid with Not Just a
Head, Blood Thinners, Boulderdash, Stay Vigilant and the Wardens' Acceleration, Penetration and
both Overclockeds. This file is the audit of our ToA against it: hitpoints, damage and each
invocation the raid ran. Everything below was checked against index 4292 (rev 241, raid level
160) and the rev 234-235 sessions where they show the same thing.

## Invocations

`tools/rsprox/toa_raid_invocations.py` (new) decodes the party panel. Clientscript 6729's two
masks are **31-bit**: the first holds indices 0-30, the second 31 onward. Read as 32-bit, every
invocation from Keep Back (31) up names its neighbour, and the panel's level stops being the
sum of the cache's levels (here 355 against 310). With the 31-bit split, the sum equals the
panel in all 50 recorded raids. The room write-ups already used the right sets; only
[nexus.md](nexus.md)'s note was wrong. Our lobby always sent 31-bit masks
(`~toa_lobby_client_mask`).

4323 (level 310): Softcore Run, Need Some Help?, Walk the Path, Deadly Prayers, On a Diet;
Kephri: Lively Larvae, More Overlords, Blowing Mud, Aerial Assault; Zebak: Not Just a Head,

## Damage

**The path factor multiplies.** Ours added raid and path, then capped at 250%. Two hits in
this raid cannot come from that:

| Hit | Recorded | raid + path (242%) | raid × path (224% × 118%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zebak's magic split (16), unprayed [3051] | 42 | max 38 | max 42 |
| A Crondis trap (4-7 × factor) [2839] | 13 | 9, 12, 14, 16 | 10, **13**, 15, 18 |

Index 3411 (raid 500, Crondis path 3) took 17 from a trap: 250% × 118% gives 11, 14, 17, 20.
Index 4292 (raid 160, path 1) took 8: 164% × 108% gives 7, 8, 10, 12. So
`~toa_damage_factor` is now min(raid factor, 250%) × path factor, the way hitpoints combine.
The cap is on the raid factor only: a 250% product would cap Zebak at 40. Path levels still
stop at 4 here.

**Akkha's prayed basics.** 25 June 2025: "protection prayers will now reduce more damage from
Akkha's standard attacks." The 9% ours used was fitted on Leagues V (December 2024). Prayed
ranged and magic hits since then: 4292 (max 40) 1 ×15, 2 ×5; 4323 (max 53) 1 ×6, 2 ×7, never
a 3. floor(roll × p) fits best at **6%** (log-likelihood −21.9; 5% −25.4, 7% −26.5, 9% −36.8).
Melee stays 15%: 4323's prayed melee ran 1-7 against a 53 max.

**Deadly Prayers**: floor(hit / 5) off prayer on the hit's tick (42 → 8, 29 → 5, 40 → 8,
26 → 5); the stray +1s are the recorder's own prayer drain. As ours.

Matched: the Wardens' phase-one balls (Elidinis 40, Tumeken 26, fixed: 18 and 12 × 2.24);
phase three's slam 35 (ours 20-40); prayed P2 shots 1-8.

## Open

- **The obelisk's sweep at Penetration**: 22 and 24 at 310 [3601, 3616], below ours (13 ×
  2.24 + 0-2 = 29-31). Two hits. Leagues V's 30-33 at the cap fit 12-13. Not refitted.
- **Ba-Ba's rubble** takes a 12 every time it shields a throw (13 of 13 in 4292 and 4323, at
  both raid levels), and its bar stays full unless that kills it; ours takes 20.
- **Akkha's enrage orbs** hit 20, 23, 20 through Protect from Magic; ours is a fixed 21 at 310.
  The orb has a random part we do not model.
- **Akkha's first special** came after 3 swings here (2 in 4292 and 3413); ours waits the full
  cycle from his first swing. Here he chased a kiting raider for 80 ticks first.
- **Jungle Japes' rotations**: 4 of the 6 pairs share one; ours always does.
- Party pools for the seal and the palm are still rev 227's.

## Tests

leak and Het window checks are updated to these numbers. It had been failing at HEAD on
"special every seven" (the special has been every eighth since rev 234). Past that it
exceeded the 500,000-instruction script cap, so its last three checks run on the next tick.

on a mirror click (`click_loc toa_het_mirror`: no copy at the tile). `toa_apmeken_tells` ends
at the frame cap, at raid level 0, where the thrall change does nothing. Neither failure
comes from these changes.
