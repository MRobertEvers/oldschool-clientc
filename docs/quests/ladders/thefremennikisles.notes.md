# The Fremennik Isles -- what the ladder cannot know (driven 2026-10-02)

Doors and reach
- Legs are cut by ROUTE (thefremennikisles.legs: 0-60, 90-150, 160-210, 230-275, 280-290, 300-; vm-b1-seam3); leg 1 starts from ::fremennikisles at stage 0.
- Jatizso, leg 1: from the dock 2420,3782 walk to 2412,3796, open frisd_outer_city_wall_door_left (2413,3797), walk to 2407,3807 and open frisd_town_wall_door (2407,3806): King Gjuki's hall is entered from the NORTH; walking straight north from the gate stalls at the jester chest (2407,3800).
- Mawnis's hall is entered from the EAST (door guards Kjedelig/Trogen at 2338,3798/3800); goto 2341,3799 first.
- The jester chest (2407,3800) is clicked from the throne-room side (stand on 2407,3801); from outside it is "can't reach".
- Ferry landings: Jatizso 2420,3782; Neitiznot 2311,3782; Rellekka 2644,3709. Maria in Rellekka ferries only from stage 50.
- Bridges: stand just south of frisb_bridge_3_s (2314,3840) / _4_s (2355,3840), op2 Repair; the repair carries you north (z 3848). The north-east bridge (_5) stays broken until the quest ends.
- East cave trapdoor (2401,3889) is entered from the WEST tile 2400,3889; the other two cave mouths only say "body of one of Jatizso's guards".
- Arctic pines are chopped from the tile south-west of them (e.g. 2314,3789 for the tree at 2315,3788); the trees at 2337,3811 and 2351,3805 cannot be reached.

Dialogue that gates progress
- Slug: "Free stuff please." then "I am ready." (outfit worn AND weapon/shield slots empty, else "Not dressed like that").
- Slug report 1: two days / seventeen / two bridges. Report 2: Etceteria / some potions / I have been helping Neitiznot. Any wrong answer = no payment, ask again.
- Tax: the amount is TYPED. Window: Flosi or Keepa 5000, Vanligga 5000, Skuli 6000, Hring or Raum 8000 (the building-mate then refuses). Beard: Raum, Hring, Skuli, Keepa, Flosi 1000 each, Vanligga none. Vanligga's 2nd option pays her 5000 yourself (opens her yak shop).
- Mawnis at stage 90 needs the jester outfit OFF; the decree dialogue (270) too.

What the player does
- Jester act: ten instructions, one every 8 ticks, any wrong button or a miss = "Useless fool!" and the act is re-asked at Mawnis. The server var varp7215_fris_jester_want holds the pending action (1 talk 2 dance 3 juggle 4 skip 5 pie 6 jig 7 bow).
- Rope: yak hair (yaks drop hair, hide, meat, bones in the field west of the village) spun at 2352,3794 (Crafting 30). The 8 ropes and 8 split logs are SHOWN to Mawnis and kept; the bridges then use 4+4 each.
- Split logs and the shield are made on iznot_shield_stump (2342,3807): use an arctic pine log on it, pick Split logs or Neitiznot shield (Woodcutting 56).
- Armour: Thakkrad cures hides (5 gp); needle on a cured hide asks body (2 hides, Crafting 46) or legs (1 hide, 43).

Fights
- Ten trolls: only cave kills count. The baby trolls by 2406,10259 respawn and are safe to farm from 2405,10258; wait in Bork's camp (2393,10288). Standing near 2413,10256 or the bodyguards (2413,10265) killed a 99-hp character twice.
- King: 150 hp, melee/rocks/magic/knock-back; a Neitiznot shield cuts rocks to 2. Bring about 17 lobsters. He is hand-spawned when bridge 6 is crossed.

Different from the guide
- Honour guards and Bork stand in the cave but do not fight; the shield does not cap ordinary trolls' rocks; prayer is not blocked while repairing. Cutscenes are a separate spec.
