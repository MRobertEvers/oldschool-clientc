Olaf's Quest notes (parity b53; source = wiki oldid 15355207 + Quest Helper, no LostCity copy)

Gate: The Fremennik Trials done (varp347_viking), Woodcutting 50, Firemaking 40.
Olaf stands at 2724,3729. Ingrid 2670,3662 (guide says 2670,3670). Volf 2659,3698.
Ingrid gives bread, Volf gives a shark; either is the "food" Olaf asks for.
Olaf asks for food after the fire (stage 40): the option "Alright, here, have some food.
Now give me the map." takes one food (olaf_hradson.rs2 olaf_after_fire); the other option
or no food still gives the map. Stage goes 40 -> 50.
Fire: damp planks on the embers (oplocu olaf_multi_fire), then tinderbox. Two uses.
Dig: spade beside the tree at 2748,3732 (spade.rs2 hook, olaf_try_dig) drops you into the cavern.
Skeletons (lvl 40/50/60 variants) drop ONE random key of five shapes; only while you hold none.
Picture wall (2707,10147): needs a key in the pack to open the lever interface. Levers right,
top, left, bottom, then confirm; the state is mod 5 so extra pulls also work.
Barrels (2710-2711,10157) and ropes (2700,10163; 2703,10155; 2718,10163) are ground items; each
tile has two obj variants, so 6 ropes / 4 barrels exist. Each repair needs 1 barrel + 3 ropes.
Walkway fall (olaf_dungeon.rs2 olaf_walkway_crossing): rolled on each barrel repair and at the
gate, success = Agility*0.86 + 32.47 + falls*2.4 percent (wiki). A fall puts you back in the room
before the walkway; 78 Agility never falls. Port rolls per action, not per tile walked.
Gate (2725,10168): the right lock button is the key's shape (cross=key_1, square=key_2,
triangle=key_3, circle=key_4, star=key_5); a wrong lock snaps the key. The gate loc does not
visually open; opening teleports you to the wreck (collapse of the walk).
Chest (2740,10164): first search spawns Ulfric (lvl 100, melee; fight needs food/prayer).
Second search after the kill gives 4 rubies, 20000 coins, 12000 Defence XP.
Not ported: the parchment reward, the map and note viewer interfaces.
