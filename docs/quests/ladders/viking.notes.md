# The Fremennik Trials -- what the ladder cannot know

Source: LostCity quest_viking, OSRS where noted. Triggers fire on the multinpc PARENT
(talk_to "viking_brundt", "viking_askelapen"), not the _child shell.

Starts: Brundt "Do you have any quests?" then "I want to become a Fremennik!" (stage 1).
Each trial adds one vote to %viking; Brundt finishes at 7 (stage 10).

Olaf: tree (2739,3639) needs 40 Woodcutting, knife on branch needs 40 Crafting, wool on
lyre 25 Fletching. Askeladden gives the pet rock FREE (Olaf_progress 2). Lalli's cauldron
takes rock, cabbage, potato, onion by use-on; then talk Lalli for the fleece. Spin it at
any wheel (viking_spinningwheel 2617,3659; skillmulti cell a). Raw shark/manta/turtle on
the altar (2626,3598). Stage door is entered from the EAST (stand 2670,3683); play the
lyre on the stage (x2655-2662, z3682-3685).

Manni: keg at (2660,3676), beer tankard (2658,3676). Council workman (2654,3593) swaps a
beer for the firecracker; tinderbox lights it (~7-10 s fuse); put it in the pipe (2663,3674);
low-alcohol keg (250 gp, poison salesman, "Talk about the Fremennik Trials") used on the
beerkeg. Then talk Manni with the keg.

Sigmund chain: 13 npcs there and back, one dialogue each, "Ask about the Merchant's
trial". Askeladden's promise costs 5000 gp. Peer is reachable only from the NORTH
(2634,3671). Items: song, boots, note, hunting map, bowstring, fish, map, forecast,
contract, token, cocktail, promise note, flower.

Sigli: talisman use points toward a hidden anchor, reveals at <=3 tiles, moves every 80
ticks, 1000 tick life (OSRS wiki The Draugen). Draugen 60 hp, magic 1.

Thorvald: the entrance is the GROUND ladder viking_warrior_ladder, op2 Climb-down at
(2667,3694,0); no weapons, armour, runes, logs or fletching stock (Peer banks all for
you). Koschei appears 20-70 ticks later beside you. Forms 1-3 die, form 4 cannot be
killed: reaching 1 hp is the safe death and the vote. Exit ladder viking_warrior_ladder_up.

Swensen: ladder (2644,3657) in, seven portals in order (2631,10002) (2639,10015)
(2656,10004) (2665,10018) (2630,10023) (2656,10037) (2666,10029), ladder (2665,10037) out.

Peer: bank everything first; door riddle on interface seer_combolock (OSRS) with the
four wheels; inside: up ladder (2631,3663), bookcase herring, bull disk, herring on the
range = goop, goop on disk, unicorn = OLD red disk (OSRS keeps two disks), trapdoor
(2636,3663,2), both disks on the mural (2634,3663,0) = lid, back up, bucket 5 (cupboard),
jug 3 (chest), 4 litres in the scale chest, vase from tap, lid on, vase on the frozen
table, key on range, trapdoor, door2.

Differences from the guide step list: none dropped; the shoe-colour panel is a dialogue
menu (the 239 cache has no shoestore interface).
