Herman waits at the gate (2345,3651); the swan_hole at (2344,3651) only opens at stage 40
 and needs a log, tinderbox, 5 iron bars and hammer in the pack, else a message.
Entering teleports to (2343,3657): 3 swan_troll_ambush; the third kill sets stage 50.
Sea trolls drop normal bones (npc default death_drop). Pick up 7 for Malignius.
The real Herman is inside at (2354,3683); the gate npc is a multinpc.
Franklin (2341,3667): firebox (2344,3676) takes a log, then the tinderbox, in that order.
The press makes iron sheets from bars once the firebox is lit; walls 1-5 take one sheet each + hammer.
Arnold (2330,3691 in the spawn file): gives a small net. Fish at swan_fish (NW corner),
 the first cast spawns a troll that must be killed; cook at swan_stove; hand in 5.
Stage 80 -> Frumscone (basement ladder at Yanille) -> Malignius at (2991,3269).
Malignius: needs 7 bones in the pack; the first talk gives a brown apron.
Master Crafter (2937,3290): only instructs. He gives NO pot or lid (changed 2026-10-03):
 make a pot and a pot lid on a potter's wheel, fire both, use the lid on the pot.
Malignius takes the airtight pot + 7 bones (consumed) and gives one swan_army (bone seeds).
Herman at 140: bone seeds -> 160; without them "I've lost the army!" sends you back to
 Malignius (bring another airtight pot + 7 bones; works until the fight starts).
"I'm ready. Let's fight!" spends the bone seeds and spawns the Sea Troll Queen at (2347,3704).
 The siege cutscene is NOT ported (spec pending): the fight is 1-on-1, no skeleton army.
Queen needs melee/ranged gear and food; her death sets 190, Herman finishes it.
