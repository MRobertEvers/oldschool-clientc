What Lies Below -- driving notes (source: wiki + Quest Helper; LostCity has no such quest)

Stage numbers: the port uses its own ladder (10,20,30,50,60,70,80,150), not the cache's 10..140.
Outlaw camp: only 3 outlaw types are spawned by ~wlb_spawn_outlaws (whatliesbelow_papers.rs2:12), they respawn on death while stage 10.
Papers drop only while folder + held papers < 5.
Folder: use a paper on the folder (opheldu); the full folder goes to Rat.
Chaos altar: Use the wand on the altar (oplocu chaos_altar, whatliesbelow_surok.rs2): needs Runecraft 35, 15 chaos runes, talisman/tiara.
Surok at stage 70: sends you away (teleport to Zaff) until Zaff has given the ring.
King fight is a controlled npc (surok_king, 75 hp, death_drop null): hits stop at 5 hp, then Operate the ring (opheld3).
Ring before 5 hp: Zaff gives up and the fight resets.
Cutscene (room darkens, Zaff scene): spec pending, 2 scenes in docs/quests/cutscenes/; not ported.
Zaff's shop opens from his Talk-to (yes) and Trade.

Tunnel of Chaos shortcut (quest reward, whatliesbelow_tunnel.rs2):
Anna Jones stands at 3285,3468 beside the statue (cache-placed locs: statue 3283,3468).
After the quest she gives a bronze pickaxe; Excavate the statue (needs a bronze pickaxe), then Enter.
Tunnel start 3179,5192; the portal is at the far NW end, 3152,5233 (about 21 ticks of walking).
Portal needs chaos talisman/tiara/RC cape/catalytic tiara and lands on the maze level 1 at 2270,4836.
Same tile holds the exit portal back to the tunnel.
The portal landing tile and Anna's dialogue wording are from the wiki summary, not a transcript.
