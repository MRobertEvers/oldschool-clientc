- Vertida is in the hideout north room (3630,9643); Safalaan on the castle wall (3584,3331), later at the base (3627,9646); Sarius appears at (3575,3331) only after Vanstrom's five blows.

What stands between two steps
- Boat deck (3604,3161,1) is cut off by water: click the rock (3605,3163) to jump (works from 3 tiles), then Climb-up it; the way back is Climb-down, the boat edge, the moored boat.
- Floorboards at (3589,3173,1): Search, Yes to kick, then ordinary Climb-down. Below them the wall breach is crossed by Climb-over on the rubble (3589,3180) or (3591,3180).
- Course: jump floorboards are clicked from the far side (they work from 3 tiles). Push walls leave board floors: click the floor (Walk-across). Pots (3608,3222) give the key for the door (3608,3221), used once.
- Agility 25 unboosted for every course obstacle. The hideout wall (3640,3253): knife on it, then Push from the north side; then press the decorated wall (3638,3251), Open the rug, Climb-down.
- Rocky surface (3592,3211) opens the barricade pass (3589,3215) for 40 ticks.
- Lab: tapestry needs the knife, statue the key, then the door (3641,3307) opens (locked before); stairs (3643,3304).

Dialogue that gates
- Ral wants the option "Old Man Ral, the sage of Sanguinesti."; the Vyrewatch and miners offer "Send me to the mines." (then confirm) and "Do you have a spare pick?".
- Roald's "Talk to the king about Morytania." Safalaan's "Okay, lead the way." (needs 4 free slots first).

Wandering and fights
- Vyrewatch hover and Vertida drifts a tile; the Vyrewatch are set huntmode=none (the generated block made them lethal).
- Vanstrom: bound to his five blows (8 ticks apart), cannot be hurt, Protect from Melee zeroes the damage. Bring 60+ hitpoints.
