# Troubled Tortugans: what the ladder cannot know

Sea: the Remote Island mooring is on its EAST side (2972,2602). Rocks block the
south approach (2949-2958,2585-2592); sail up x=2975 from z=2545 to 2593. Leave
the Summer Shore plank (3174,2367) by 3178,2340 then west along z=2335.
Little Pearl: mooring 3354,2216 faces north; park the hull at about 3354,2226.
Boarding the island mooring uses op 1 (stage 8 -> 10); op 2 is sailing's.
Arrival (stage 10 -> 12) moves when the plank is clicked with the HULL near
3174,2367, not when the boat arrives.

Floopa: one spawn on the island (2962,2605). Korel and docks Floopa stand on the
pier at 3185,2371. Raley (3186,2405) and house Floopa come from redreef.spawn.
Talking to Floopa or Raley in the house starts the same scene.

Dialogue gates: "Yes." starts the quest (stage 2 then 4). Raley's list needs a
free slot; with a full pack the stage stays 16 and he offers it again. The
three questions after the repairs loop until "I'd better get to it."

Repairs: the six locs are repairable only at stage 18 (varbit 1 = broken, 2 =
fixed). Needs hammer AND saw in the pack (hammer at 3177,2428, saw at 3187,2404),
plus 6 scutes, 6 shells, 10 jatoba logs in total (jatoba trees west of town,
level 40). The sixth repair writes stage 20 by itself.

Trail: monument 22 -> 24, then plant 3128,2423, rockslide 3127,2447, plant
3137,2468, plant 3151,2474 in that order (other plants of the same kind say
"Nothing seems out of place"). The cave needs an axe in the pack or worn.

Fights: Gryphon (cave, 110 hp, max hit 8): melee, Protect from Melee stops it;
worn weight under 45 kg lets its slam land. Shellbane (400 hp, max 22): wear the
tortugan shield or prayers are ignored; melee beside it, ranged otherwise; the
elders chip it to 20 hp over time. Bring food. Both are shared-world npcs added
on entry, not instances.

Different from the guide: no camera cutscene on arriving at the Pearl (spec
pending); Floopa riding the boat is only the stage value; the Pearl fight has no
whirlwinds or spit; Blunn's shield is one-off via varb18333.
