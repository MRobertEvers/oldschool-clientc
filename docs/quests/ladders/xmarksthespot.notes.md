# X Marks the Spot - author notes

Veos stands at 3228,3242 inside The Sheared Ram. Stand at 3229,3242 to talk
(the ::xmarksthespot cheat drops you ON his tile; talk_to then says "can't reach").
Accepting the scroll hides the Lumbridge Veos for good (varb12151 = 3):
after step 1 talk to veos_sarim at 3054,3245, not veos_lumbridge.

Start menu is three rows (Who are you? / I'm looking for a quest. / I have to go.);
then "Can I help?" and "Sounds good, what should I do?". A full backpack
sets stage 1 and gives no scroll; talk again for "Ready to help".

Digs: opheld1 on the spade at the exact tile (xmarksthespot.rs2 xmarks_try_dig_quiet).
Bob 3230,3209; castle 3203,3212; Draynor 3109,3264; pig pen 3078,3259.
No door or gate lies on the way if you ::goto; the Draynor jail guards are aggressive on foot.
The orb answers by Chebyshev distance: 0 incredibly hot, <=3 very hot, <=8 hot,
<=15 warm, <=30 cold, else freezing.

Clue 1 and the cipher open the trail clue page; clue 2 opens the cache map
interface (cluequest_map). The casket dig plays the whisper dialogue
(objbox, then two player lines). Lost clue/orb: talk to Veos in stages 2-5.
Lost casket (stage 6): dig the pen again.

Different from the guide: the reward is the beginner CLUE scroll (the 2025
wiki change to a scroll box is not ported; the box has no open mechanic).
The antique lamp Rubs through the shared xpreward picker (twocats.rs2 lamp kind 8,
300 XP, any skill). The "visited Kourend before" dialogue variant is not built.
No fights, no wandering npcs.
