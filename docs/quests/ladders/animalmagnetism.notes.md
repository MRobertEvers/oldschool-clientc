# Animal Magnetism: what the ladder cannot know

Source: OSRS wiki + Quest Helper. LostCity has no copy, so there is no LostCity table.

Malcolm (anma_ghost_farmer_multi) is a shell spawn at 3618,3526 from anma.spawn.
Alice is the world spawn at 3627,3526. Both need the ghostspeak amulet worn.
The Old crone (ahoy_crone) is shared with Ghosts Ahoy. If both quests are live
she asks which one you mean: pick "I'm here about Alice and her husband."
The crone amulet: stage 70 talk, 73 talk again, she hands over the amulet.
Malcolm takes it (choice "Okay, you need it more than I do"), then the chicken
scene plays as a mesbox only (no camera, spec pending) and stage 100 follows.
Chickens cost 10 ecto-tokens each; bring 20.
Alice: "I'm here about a quest." moves 20 to 30. Her shop is option 2 in the
same menu, or Trade (op 3). Both open farming_shop_4 (shop/alices_farm).
The witch wants 5 iron bars, hands back one Selected iron (anma_iron_bar).
Hammer it inside the Rimmington mine box (anma.constant anma_rimm_mine_*).
The guide says face north; the port does not check facing (no facing query).
Undead trees: state 150 swaps the multinpc to the choppable form. The first
chop with a mithril axe bounces (150 -> 160). Turael (Burthorpe) needs two
talks: intro (170), then the axe swap; mithril axe and holy symbol are consumed
by the exchange (anma_trees.rs2:72). Twigs: 30 percent fail chance per chop.
Research notes: nine toggles in the notes interface; all on (511) solves it.
Pattern + hard leather + polished buttons: use-on in anma_notes.rs2:130.
Stage 240 gives 1 qp and 1000/1000/1000/2500 xp as the scroll shows.
