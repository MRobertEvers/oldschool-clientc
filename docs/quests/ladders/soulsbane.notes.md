A Soul's Bane - what the ladder cannot know (driven end to end 2026-10-02, 170 rows green)

State bands (Quest Helper): odd = previous room done, even = inside that room's attempt.
1 rope, 2 rage, 3 rage done, 4 fear, 5 fear done, 6 confusion, 7 confusion done,
8 hopeless, 9 hopeless done, 10 Tolna's room, 11 heads dead, 12 Tolna on the surface, 13 done.

Entry: use a rope on the rift at 3310,3452, then Enter it. Rope entry puts you in the room
of your checkpoint (state 1-2 rage, 3-4 fear, 5-6 confusion, 7-8 hopeless, 9 bridge copy,
10-11 Tolna's room, 13 the lobby 3104,5279). At 12 the rope refuses.
Each room's small void (its entry tunnel) asks "Do you wish to leave?" and returns to the rift.

Rage: rack 3012,5243 offers sword/spear/mace/battleaxe/Nothing. Unicorn=sword, bear=spear,
rat=mace, goblin=battleaxe. WIELD it. Matching weapon hits x10, anything else 0. 8 kills of the
matching animal (each death spawns a fresh one), no combat xp, 40 Attack xp at the end.
The fire at the east exit 3038,5228 drops when the bar is full. Anger weapons are removed on any exit.

Fear: six holes, one hides the reaper (varb2012, the hole index 0-5, re-rolled after each kill).
A wrong hole just shouts. Reaper level 42, melee. Five kills light the exit at 3046,5236.

Confusion: five beasts per wave, one real. Illusions take 0 and vanish after 8 hits (still pay
combat xp, melee only - ranged/magic bypass that hook). The real one's death removes a door and
brings a new wave. Five waves, then door 6 at 3051,5200.

Hopelessness: five creatures, three forms each (monst3, 2, 1), 15 kills. The Bridge of Hope is
the WEST copy of the room (cache keeps it 64 tiles west): the fifth kill carries you to x-64.
Walk to the exit 3020,5188 (it can sit under the interface - turn the camera or walk first).
The east exit 3084,5188 only works once the bridge is up and also carries you west.

Tolna's room (plane 1): Brana speaks, the heads wake. Heads at 2984/2981/2978,5212, level 46,
melee. All three dead -> human Tolna at 2984,5212. Talk to him (walk to 2981,5212 first, he can
be off the camera) and you are carried to the surface; Launa is hidden, Tolna is the surface npc.
Surface hand-in needs one free slot or a coin stack; it pays 500 coins, 500 Defence, 500 Hitpoints.

Different from the guide: the rooms are the cache's fixed public squares, not private instances;
the Rage overlay is the old cache interface; cutscenes are not ported (spec pending).
Post-quest lobby rooms: quest-local configs/soulsbane.spawn, no drops tuning.
