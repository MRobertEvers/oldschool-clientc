Gates between steps

- Hazelmere needs UNBOOSTED 46 Magic and a free slot (Violet pentagon). Below 46 the talk ends at stage 5; the whole story is ~90 pages and sets stage 15 only at the end.
- Repair: use the glue on the machine or click it. First click shows the damage text (stage 22); then every missing part is named. Construction 5, hammer, saw, oak, maple, glue. Glue = pestle on mud rune, then ground runes on the sap bucket.
- Unlock window (145): click a disc in the right-hand panel to pick it up, click the slot, press Unlock. Operate window (189): rows of 1, 2, 3 discs; all six slots full and each row's sum equal to its number, then any Activate button.
- Value = colour (red 1 .. violet 7) x sides (circle 1, triangle 3, square 4, pentagon 5). Targets are rolled once per player (eyeglo_roll_targets) as sums of Brimstail's pool, so they are solvable from his discs.
- Brimstail: stage 25 gives Red square + Yellow triangle; "I can't work out..." gives 3 random discs (never red, never circle) and refuses at 6 or more carried. Drop and ask again.
- Exchanger (111): up to 35 points in, Exchange re-rolls the offer every press, Accept takes it, Cancel returns the originals.
- Creatures are Cute until the machine runs, then attackable; they do not retaliate. Kills count per player; tell King Narnode at 45. Narnode's first talk at 36-44 sets eyeglo_switch_1.
