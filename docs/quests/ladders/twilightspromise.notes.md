# Twilight's Promise: what the ladder cannot know (driven 2026-10-03)

Target the spawned BASE of every multinpc, never the leaf: vmq2_ennius_outer_palace /
vmq2_furia_outer_palace (1687,3141, stages 0-4), vmq2_ennius_inner_palace (1684,3156,
12-38), vmq2_metzli_fortis, vmq2_itzla_fortis (crypt), vmq2_knight_1_bazaar,
vmq2_knight_3_cothon, vmq2_knight_4_pub, vmq2_knight_5_follower, vmq2_knight_6_colosseum,
vmq2_citizen, quetzal_fortis (Renu), vmq2_itzla_teomat, vmq2_metzli_teomat.

Where things really stand and how you reach them
- Varrock Regulus only shows after Children of the Sun (first_travel 1).
- Crypt: stairs top 1692,3088 land you at 1693,9493. The gate at 1698,9496 says "securely
  locked" until Metzli has sent you (stage 8), then opens normally. The prince is 1685,9514.
- Renu stands in a pen (invisible walls): reach her from the SOUTH, 1703,3141. Regulus is
  inside it too; talk to him from 1699,3141.
- Pub: the bar counter runs along z 3076; stand south of it (1723,3074).
- Colosseum entrance: click it from 1797,3106. Mezan is in the lobby at 1805,9522.
- Kualti HQ: ground-floor door on the EAST wall (1657,3150); stairs inside at 1638,3155,
  then 1650,3155 on floor 1. North of z 3156 is outside the building.
- Teomat: the flight lands at 1437,3171; the altar room (Itzla) is 1452,3173; Metzli
  is in the north building at 1448,3196.

Dialogue gates
- Every knight group refuses without the crest; Furia/Ennius give another if lost.
- Reading the letter (not just finding it) starts the twins' judgement.
- Feeding Renu needs the feed from Regulus, then the flight menu opens by itself.

Fights
- Mezan: lvl 81, 100 hp, max 8. Bring two styles AND the ammo equipped. After four swings in
  one style he prays against it (shout "Ralos will defend me!"); swing in another style.
  Poison still works. Fought in your own copy of the arena; the gate loc leaves it.
- Cultists: 8, lvl 34, 25 hp, max 4, in your own copy of the Teomat square. One attacks, the
  rest wait until hit. Die or leave: talk to Metzli again at stage 44 to re-enter.

Forms that differ from the guide
- Azali follows on foot and stops at random (knights.rs2:364): talk to her again. A teleport
  breaks it; walk. Leading her 60+ tiles from the pub sends her home.
- Citizen pickpocket: Thieving 1, 8 xp; the amulet only while the bazaar knights have asked.
- The letter chest is random per player (varp7290): vmq2_knight_4_chest or _1_chest.
- Guards do not fight the cultists; the player kills all eight (battle.rs2:1-15).
- Mezan's prayer is a rule in the hit funnel (colosseum.rs2:102), no head icon.
- The three cutscenes (fountain dunk, Velam's execution, Teomat aftermath) are dialogue only.
