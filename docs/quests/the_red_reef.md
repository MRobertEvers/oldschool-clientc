## Requirements
Sailing 52 and Smithing 48 (not boostable); Troubled Tortugans (and so
Pandemonium). Combat 65 and Prayer 40 recommended.

## Walkthrough (the legs content models)
1. Elder Raley (house south of the Summer Shore bank) -> a tortugan in the
   marketplace (Quest Helper: Finn) -> Elder Katt grants the Sacred Grove ->
   Floopa inside.
2. Sail to Red Rock (far west of the Great Conch); receptionist; the four
   display cases in chronological order, nearest the door first and then
   anti-clockwise; Theodore Paxton sends you after Black Eye Bethel.
3. Sailing south to Last Light: two pirate ships (Bark of the Bight, Four of a
   Kind), 250 hitpoints and four crew each; sink them with cannons or kill all
   crew with magic/ranged.
4. Last Light: six pirates (four outside, two inside), then two flights of
   stairs to Black Eye Bethel (level 191; charge dodge, rune then dragon
   scimitar below 70% / 40%, prayer-disable special).
5. Back to Paxton, who refers you to his boss Spencer Brentwood on the Zenith
   (east of Red Rock). Spencer provides the diving equipment if you lack it;
   underwater, repairing the northern coral dredger brings a giant lobster
   (level 112); then the south-eastern dredger where Spencer waits.
6. Spencer reveals the red coral is running out and he wants the Pearl Bank
   near the Little Pearl -- the company was behind the attack on the island.
   Return to Elder Raley's house, where Floopa now is.

## Rewards
2 Quest points; 15,000 Sailing experience; 5,000 Smithing experience; Red Rock
on the Sailor's amulet (Sailors' Marker); access to the Sacred Grove; Bosun's
workbench schematic.

## Known divergences in the port (parity3f)
Ships are modelled as eight crew npcs, not hulls; Bethel's charge/weapon-swap/
prayer mechanics are not scripted; the dialogue is paraphrased and misses the
story beats of step 6 (Floopa's history with Spencer, Spencer's Pearl Bank
plan) -- a transcript pass is owed.
