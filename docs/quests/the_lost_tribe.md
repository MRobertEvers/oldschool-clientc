## Requirements
Goblin Diplomacy, Rune Mysteries; Agility 13, Thieving 13, Mining 17
(boostable); a pickaxe and a light source.

## Walkthrough
1. Cook, Hans, Father Aereck and Bob: one of them saw "a goblin with huge eyes
   in the castle basement" (content: always Bob).
2. Duke's permission; mine the cellar rubble (Mining 17), squeeze through
   (Agility 13), take the brooch.
3. Show the Duke; Reldo in the Varrock Palace library; "A History of the
   Goblin Race" on the western bookcase; reading it identifies the Dorgeshuun
   symbol.
4. The Goblin Village generals teach the goblin bow and salute.
5. The tunnels with a lit light source: follow the rock symbols; "wrong paths
   may result in the player either taking damage or falling into the Swamp
   Caves" (whose gas ignites open flames).
6. Goblin Bow to Mistag; he arranges peace.
7. Pickpocket Sigmund (Thieving 13) for the key, his chest holds H.A.M. robes;
   the silverware from the H.A.M. Hideout to the Duke; the treaty to Ur-tag and
   the signing ceremony.

## Rewards
1 Quest point; 3,000 Mining experience; Goblin Bow and Goblin Salute emotes;
the Dorgesh-Kaan mine; Ring of life; Nardok's bone weapon store; a mining
helmet.
