# In Search of the Myreque -- wiki brief (pinned for the parity3f pass)

Source: https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/In_Search_of_the_Myreque (read 2026-09-30) and the
pinned revisions listed in OSRS-Content .../quest_routequest/configs/quest_routequest.constant.
Port source: LostCity (LostCity_Server/content/scripts/quest_routequest); the OSRS form wins below.

- Requires Nature Spirit (Priest in Peril, The Restless Ghost before it), 25 Agility (boostable),
  the ability to defeat a level 97 Skeleton Hellhound (safespottable; no Magic defence).
- Items: steel longsword, 2 steel swords, steel mace, steel warhammer, steel dagger; 225 steel
  nails; 6 planks; a druid pouch with 5 charges; a hammer; 10 coins or a Ring of Charos (a).
- Walkthrough: Vanstrom Klause (Hair of the Dog, Canifis) -> Cyreg Paddlehorn (Mort'ton; the four
  persuasion options; he takes 3 planks for the boat, the other 3 are for the bridge) -> pay 10
  coins (or the ring) and board the Swamp Boaty -> The Hollows -> climb the tree north of the boat
  -> mend the rope bridge, one plank and 75 nails a section -> Curpile Fyod asks three of six
  questions (female: Sani Piliu; leader: Veliaf Hurtz; boatman: Cyreg Paddlehorn; scholar: Polmafi
  Ferdygris; youngest: Ivan Strom; ruling family: Drakan) -> the earth doors -> the hidden cave ->
  introduce yourself to the five members, give Veliaf the weapons -> Vanstrom's cutscene (mist, he
  kills Sani and Harold, summons the hellhound) -> kill it -> Veliaf tells the way out -> the
  false wall and the ladder behind the inn -> the Stranger in the pub finishes the quest.
- Rewards: 2 Quest Points; 600 XP each in Attack, Defence, Strength, Hitpoints, Crafting; access
  to The Hollows and fairy ring DLS; the quick route to Mort'ton (Quick-board).
- OSRS-era details kept over LostCity: 25 Agility, 75 nails a rung (LostCity: 10), druid pouch
  needs 5 charges, planks handed over with "Here are some planks for you.", "Start the quest?"
  prompt, "Let's talk about the weapons." on Veliaf, Quick-board, a hellhound that drops nothing.
