panel_icon.png -- where it came from.

The XP Tracker's rail icon is the OldSchool popout's own graphic, not
RuneLite's similar `skill_icons/overall.png` resource.

`torirs_popout_icons` (clientscript 5356) looks up each popout slot in
`enum_4067`, then assigns `struct_param(slot, param_1412)` as its graphic. Slot
1 is XP Tracker (`struct_3742`), whose `param_1412` is 3579: `popout_icons,2`.

The cache sprite has a 30x30 logical canvas and 23x22 painted bounds at (3,4).
SOURCES.sh extracts that complete logical sprite so the cache-authored padding,
palette, and alpha all survive. `overview_icon.png` is intentionally different:
the XP tracker's total row names `staticons2,7` (sprite 222) directly.
